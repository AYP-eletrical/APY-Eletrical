AYP Electrical is a marketplace that connects customers with verified, qualified electricians — making it simple to find trustworthy help for any job, big or small.

Our Story

Built on trust, powered by expertise

AYP Electrical started with a simple frustration: finding a reliable electrician shouldn't be a gamble. Too many people were left waiting on no-shows, guessing at fair pricing, or unsure if the person wiring their home was even qualified to do it.

So we built a company that solves that problem directly — every electrician on our team is vetted for qualifications and track record before they ever step onto a job. Customers get transparent pricing, verified reviews, and a support team that stands behind every job.

Today, AYP connects thousands of customers with electricians across residential, commercial, solar, and emergency work — all with the same promise we started with: reliable people, done right.
